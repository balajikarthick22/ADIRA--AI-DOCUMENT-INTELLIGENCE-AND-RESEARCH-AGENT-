Refund Policy
Customers can request a refund within 30 days of purchase. Refunds are processed within 7 business days after the request is approved. Digital products are refundable only if they have not been downloaded.

Shipping Policy
Standard shipping takes 5 to 8 business days. Express shipping takes 2 business days and costs 15 dollars extra. Orders above 100 dollars ship free.

Support Hours
Customer support is available Monday to Friday, from 9 AM to 6 PM. Support is closed on public holidays.
